UNDERSTANDING BLACKROCK

GLOBAL OPERATIONS BLACKROCK

Founded in 1988, BlackRock developed from an institutional investment and risk-management business into a global asset-management company.

Its businesses span investment management, exchange-traded funds, investment technology, institutional services and private-market strategies.

GLOBAL ASSET MANAGEMENT

CAPITAL AT SCALE.

Asset managers invest money on behalf of clients including individuals, pension funds, institutions and other investors.

Assets under management describe client investments managed by a firm. They are not the same as assets owned directly by the asset manager.
