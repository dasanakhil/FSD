01 // THE MYSTERY

WHAT IS AREA 51?

Area 51 is the popular name associated with a highly restricted U.S. military installation at Groom Lake, Nevada.

The facility has long been connected with the testing and development of experimental aircraft and classified aerospace programs.

Its secrecy has also made it a central part of UFO folklore and extraterrestrial conspiracy theories.
